Welcome 👋

A quick 3D preview tool for customers, fence sellers and installers. Everything on the ground is measured in metres.

  • Home tab — pick a typical home, set land width/depth, rotate and nudge the house.
  • Fence tab — press Draw fence, then left-click on the ground to place corner points. Right-click or press Enter to close a loop.
  • Ctrl+click on a handle removes that point; Undo removes the last point.
  • Pick a style (timber / metal / concrete / chain link), height and post spacing — the fence rebuilds instantly.
  • Results tab — live fence length, enclosed area, post/panel counts, plus PNG / CSV / JSON export for quotes and installer layout.
  • Results tab — send your design to Yardex as a lead and a specialist will follow up with a quote.
